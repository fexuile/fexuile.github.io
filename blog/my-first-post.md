Why I need a blog? 
Maybe many people have this question when they set the website. For fun or for record is common. I think there are two benefits that we can gain through writing blogs:
1. Insights come quickly. It's hard for me to turn every insight into a project. I'm not Diyu, so it's important to record some sparks and then turn into outputs someday.
2. Practice your English. I'm native-speaker of Chinese, so I think write daily in Chinese is very easy to convert my thoughts. However, things go worse when I try to write in English. I can't even express my words, and my feelings. So I think this needs a long term practice.

Hope this could help you.
