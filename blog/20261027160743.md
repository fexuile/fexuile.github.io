What do we think when we make the plan? We all want to reach our goal in time. However, according to my experience, every plan will finally come to nothing. We can neither finish all nor in time. So where does the problem come from? 

1. Firstly, we overestimated our power. We can't do some many thing in restrict time.
2. Secondly, we want to rest when we finish one part. But we didn't arrange a time for rest.

Back to my life, when Diyu asked me to work at least 60h one week, I think we can split this into 6 days. Each day requires me to work 10h, starting from 8am. Morning includes 3.5h, Afternoon includes 3.5h, and evening includes 3h. It's so strange because we didn't really have a rest time. 
So I think we need to include the noon rest in 10h. 

In conclusion, we will **start working on** **8am, and end on 6pm**. The evening is all your time.
